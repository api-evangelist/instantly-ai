Cold email sequence best practices from top GTM teams: 4 to 7 steps, under 80 words per email, 3 to 7 day spacing for benchmark reply rates. Standardizing these rules across your team protects domain health while pushing replies toward the 5.5% to 10.7% top performer benchmarks.
